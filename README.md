# Portfolio-
Project presents a literature-based study on phase change materials (PCMs) and their application in thermal energy storage for building systems. The focus is on understanding how PCMs utilize latent heat during phase transitions to regulate temperature and improve thermal stability in buildings.
The study reviews key material properties, including thermal energy storage capacity, phase transition behavior, and thermal conductivity, and discusses how these properties influence their performance in construction applications. It also examines different PCM types and commonly used methods for their integration into building materials to enhance energy efficiency.
It also  focuses on the characterization and efficiency analysis of microencapsulated Phase Change Materials (PCMs) using Micrasoft Excel for data organization, performance evoluation.By analyzing various combinations of Core and Shell materials, the study evaluates critical thermal properties such as Latent Heat (J/g), Melting Temperature, and Encapsulation Efficiency.
​###Objectives: To evaluate the thermal performance of different microencapsulation forming processes.
​To identify the most efficient material combinations for energy storage applications.
​###Tools & Technologies Used
​Microsoft Excel: Advanced PivotTables for data aggregation and hierarchical grouping.
​Data Visualization: Comparative charting (Combo charts) with secondary axes for multi-variable analysis.
​Data Analysis Techniques: Statistical grouping, outlier detection, and performance benchmarking.
