# Portfolio-
This project presents a literature-based study on *Phase Change Materials (PCMs)* and their application in thermal energy storage for building systems. The focus is on understanding how PCMs utilize latent heat during phase transitions to regulate temperature and improve thermal stability in buildings.

The study reviews key material properties, including:
* Thermal energy storage capacity
* Phase transition behavior
* Thermal conductivity

It also examines different PCM types and commonly used methods for their integration into building materials to enhance energy efficiency.

### Objectives:
* *Evaluate* the thermal performance of different microencapsulation forming processes.
* *Identify* the most efficient material combinations for energy storage applications.
* *Analyze* performance evolution by comparing various combinations of Core and Shell materials.

### Tools & Technologies Used:
* *Microsoft Excel:* Advanced PivotTables for data aggregation and hierarchical grouping.
* *Data Visualization:* Comparative charting (Combo charts) with secondary axes for multi-variable analysis.
* *Data Analysis Techniques:* Statistical grouping, outlier detection, and performance benchmarking.
